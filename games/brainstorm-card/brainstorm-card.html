Card battler on a 4×4 board Made in Godot
午後激盪 A tactical card battler by Anemois and friends: draft a deck, then deploy, attack and score on a 4×4 board. Hot-seat, against the CPU, a tower run, or online. The game is in Traditional Chinese. The first start downloads the Godot engine (up to 45 MB); after that it comes from the cache.
